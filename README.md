## 💜 Why Tech For Good?

I didn't fall in love with technology because of the code.

I fell in love with it because of what it can **do.**

I've seen how the right tool in the right hands can open doors, save lives, connect communities, and give people back their dignity. I've also seen how badly tech can hurt people when it's built without care  without thinking about **who it affects.**

That's why I care about **mental health and technology.** We are building systems that touch people's most vulnerable moments — their health, their safety, their information — and that responsibility is not something I take lightly.

I want to be the kind of developer who **builds with empathy.** Always.
